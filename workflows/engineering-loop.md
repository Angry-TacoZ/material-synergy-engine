# Engineering Loop

1. Inspect before editing and reproduce the reported behavior or evidence where possible.
2. Identify the likely blast radius and the actual caller, data, asset, or render path.
3. Keep work within the approved scope; fix tightly related defects that share the root cause.
4. Verify the real result with relevant project checks and inspect changed output.
5. Report what changed, what related systems were checked, and what the evidence establishes.
