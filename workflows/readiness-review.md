# Readiness Review

1. Declare the scope being assessed: local single-user, private hosted, public low-risk, public multi-user, or sensitive/high-impact.
2. Review security, privacy, reliability, accessibility, operations, cost, recovery, and verification as relevant to that scope.
3. Treat unknown or unverified conditions as gaps.
4. Use one status: `PRODUCTION-READY FOR DECLARED SCOPE`, `CONDITIONALLY READY`, `NOT PRODUCTION-READY`, or `NOT ASSESSED`.
5. Tie every readiness claim to evidence and list blockers and remaining risks.
