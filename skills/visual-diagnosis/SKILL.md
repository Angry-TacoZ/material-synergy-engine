# Visual Diagnosis

- Classify the defect first: alignment, silhouette, contrast, scale or crop, anchor or offset, material or lighting, wrong asset instance, or stale build/cache.
- Inspect the real asset and render path before changing styles or code.
- Change one likely cause at a time when practical.
- Compare before and after at the relevant viewport and verify the visible result materially changed.
- Report any viewport or runtime that could not be checked.
